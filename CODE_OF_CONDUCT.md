# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

All contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to abide by its terms.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers directly.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.
